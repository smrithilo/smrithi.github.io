About

Robotics research grounded in learning, planning, and real systems.

I am currently pursuing an MSE in Robotics at the University of Pennsylvania and graduated from BITS Pilani in 2025 with a B.E. (Hons.) in Mechanical Engineering and a minor in Robotics and Automation.

My current work focuses on visuomotor behavioral cloning and diffusion policies for bimanual robotic manipulation. More broadly, I am interested in long-horizon autonomy, multimodal learning, world models, planning–learning interfaces, perception, and autonomous systems.

I am seeking part-time robotics, AI, and ML opportunities for Spring 2027 and full-time opportunities for Summer 2027.
