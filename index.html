About

I found my way into robotics through student robotics teams, where building real systems showed me how mechanics, software, and autonomy depend on one another. In my junior year, Prof. Ashwin K. P. [Xtent Innovations] gave me my first opportunity to turn that curiosity into research, working on kinematic models for multi-segment cable-driven continuum robots with varying cable offsets.

I carried that interest into my undergraduate thesis with Prof. Howie Choset at Carnegie Mellon’s Biorobotics Lab, shifting towards hierarchical planners for multi-agent collective construction with teams of robots. I then worked at Open Science Stack, building the autonomy infrastructure that connects perception, planning, and robot hardware.

Today, I work with Prof. Antonio Loquercio at GRASP, developing visuomotor behavioral-cloning pipelines and diffusion policies for bimanual manipulation. I am especially interested in long-horizon autonomous systems that can learn from multimodal experience, build useful world models, and stay purposeful across multi-step tasks.

I am seeking part-time robotics, AI, and ML opportunities for Spring 2027 and full-time opportunities for Summer 2027.
